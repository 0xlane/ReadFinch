These UI icon assets are bundled locally from the following public Iconify sets.
The same three icon sets also supply the complete offline command-icon sheets in ../command-icons. TextHop arranges their glyphs in a grid and namespaces internal SVG IDs. The SVG paths are unmodified. Titles and layout are implemented by TextHop.
